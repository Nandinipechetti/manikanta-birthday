My dearest Manikanta,

Sometimes I wonder if you truly know how much you mean to me.

I know I may not always say everything perfectly. There are feelings inside my heart that are much bigger than the words I know how to say.

But if there is one thing I want you to remember, it is this:

You have been my strength in more ways than you will ever know.

There have been moments when I felt tired, confused, worried or simply not strong enough. And somehow, just knowing that you were beside me made everything feel a little less frightening.

You don't always have to say something. Sometimes your presence itself is enough.

Your hand in mine. Your voice. Your smile. The simple knowledge that I have you.

Those little things mean more to me than I can explain.

I don't want a perfect life. I want a real life. A life where we laugh, where we grow, where we sometimes disagree, where we forgive, where we support each other, and where we keep choosing each other.

If life ever becomes difficult, I want you to remember that you have me too.

Just as you have been my strength, I want to be yours.

I want to stand beside you on your best days, and hold your hand on the days when you don't feel your best.

Happy Birthday to the man who became one of the most precious parts of my life.

I love you more deeply than this little website could ever explain.

Forever yours,
Bujji (Nandini) ❤️
