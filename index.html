You are the person I want to tell everything to, whether it is something wonderful, something silly, or something that made my day difficult.

But I want you to know that I will always choose to work through them with you.

Happy Birthday, my love. Thank you for being my husband and for sharing this beautiful journey with me.


I love you, today, tomorrow, and for all the days that follow. ❤️
